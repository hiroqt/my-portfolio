# Portfolio Projects Showcase

## 1. e Buddy (eGov Hackathon 2026 Entry)
- **Slug**: `e-buddy`
- **Title**: e Buddy (eGov Hackathon 2026)
- **Tagline**: Unified government services platform using an agentic AI named e Buddy.
- **Category**: AI, Government Tech, Full-Stack
- **Tags**: TypeScript, AI, Tailwind CSS, Agentic Workflows
- **Featured**: Yes (Hackathon Entry)
- **Overview**:
  e Buddy was developed as a flagship submission for the eGov Hackathon 2026. Public services across government agencies are historically fragmented, requiring citizens to navigate separate portals, repeat document verification, and queue for hours.
- **Core Features**:
  - **Agentic AI Assistant**: Multi-modal AI chat, UI widgets, and voice command system that guides citizens step-by-step through government procedures.
  - **EGOV SSO & E-Verify**: Unified Single Sign-On and digital identity verification across public sector portals.
  - **Face Live Nest**: Real-time biometric face liveness detection for secure citizen onboarding.
  - **eMessage & eGov Pay**: Secure citizen notifications and integrated public fee payment gateway.
  - **DBM Compass & eChain**: Department of Budget and Management transparency analytics and immutable record trails.
- **Architecture**: Next.js/TypeScript frontend with responsive Tailwind CSS interface, agentic LLM routing, and secure API bridging.

## 2. PaceMentor (AI Running Coach)
- **Slug**: `pacementor`
- **Title**: PaceMentor
- **Tagline**: AI-powered running coach with adaptive training plans and Strava integration.
- **Category**: Mobile App, AI, Health & Fitness
- **Tags**: Flutter, Dart, AI, Mobile
- **Featured**: Yes (AI Running Coach)
- **Overview**:
  Amateur runners often struggle to establish consistent, injury-free training routines without expensive human coaching. PaceMentor democratizes elite coaching through mobile AI.
- **Core Features**:
  - **Adaptive Training Plans**: Dynamic training schedules generated by an AI engine tailored to runner biometrics, target race distance, and fitness baseline.
  - **Real-Time GPS Tracking**: High-accuracy run tracking with automatic pause detection when walking or stopping at intersections.
  - **Strava Integration**: Two-way sync to import historical activities and automatically publish completed runs.
  - **Social Sharing**: Auto-generates shareable run recap cards optimized for Instagram Stories and social feeds.
- **Architecture**: Flutter and Dart for high-performance cross-platform iOS & Android execution with native GPS background services and AI recommendation algorithms.

## 3. Present Po (Workforce Attendance & Time Tracking)
- **Slug**: `present-po`
- **Title**: Present Po
- **Tagline**: Workforce attendance and time-tracking platform with scheduling and presence monitoring.
- **Category**: B2B SaaS, Productivity, AI
- **Tags**: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, AI
- **Featured**: Yes (B2B Solution)
- **Overview**:
  A modern B2B SaaS platform targeting the friction in traditional workforce management and student/internship time tracking (OJT).
- **Core Features**:
  - **Tap Time-In / Time-Out**: One-tap attendance logging with geofencing validation to ensure check-ins occur only within designated vicinities.
  - **AI Journaling**: Intelligent auto-completion and summarization of daily work logs and internship reflections.
  - **OCR PDF Word to Text**: Automated text extraction from uploaded certificates and timesheets.
  - **QR Code Group Management**: Fast team and student cohort onboarding via dynamic QR codes.
  - **Bulk Certification**: Automated generation of completion certificates with verified hour totals.
- **Architecture**: Next.js App Router on Vercel paired with Supabase PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, and AI processing.

## 5. Hospital Queuing System
- **Slug**: `hospital-queuing-system`
- **Title**: Hospital Queuing System
- **Tagline**: Centralized local queuing system with AI integration developed for a hospital.
- **Category**: Enterprise, Healthcare, AI
- **Tags**: Vue.js, PHP, MySQL, AI
- **Overview**:
  Built during a 486-hour internship at General Emilio Aguinaldo Memorial Hospital to resolve congested triage queues and patient routing bottlenecks.
- **Core Features**:
  - **Offline / Local Host Operation**: Designed to function reliably on a local hospital network without requiring active internet connectivity.
  - **Multi-Role Dashboards**: Super-admin, admin, and doctor/staff control panels for real-time queue management.
  - **Ticket Printing & Display Monitors**: Automated thermal ticket printing upon patient check-in and waiting room audio/visual callouts.
  - **AI Triage Assistance**: Integrated Groq LLM to provide triage estimations and routing suggestions based on patient symptoms.
- **Architecture**: Vue.js reactive frontend, PHP backend, MySQL database, local thermal printer integration, Groq LLM.
